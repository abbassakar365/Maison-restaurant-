        La maison

        Paris on Hamra Street

        Maison Hamra brings the brasserie to Beirut: onion soup under a blanket of gruyère, steak frites, bœuf bourguignon, bouillabaisse and a pâtisserie counter that changes with the seasons, served in a dining room of marble, brass and bentwood.

        Our kitchen cooks the French classics with halal beef and chicken and without a drop of alcohol, so every guest can share the whole table, from a long lunch to a late dinner.

        How reservations work

        1. Reserve online from 12:00 to 23:00 with your name, phone, guests, date and time. Fully booked times are marked in the list.
        2. We confirm: the team reviews your request and you get a notification.
        3. Deposit: set menus over $300 need a 25% deposit, paid before the deadline shown on your reservation.
        4. On the day: you and the team both get a morning reminder, and we call to give the final approval.
        5. After your meal: we ask what you loved and what we can do better.

        What we serve

        French brasserie classics: soupe à l'oignon, steak frites, bœuf bourguignon, blanquette, bouillabaisse. Sandwiches at lunch, a French-Lebanese mezza table to share, house pastries, coffee, fresh juices and mocktails.

        Alcohol-free

        Maison Hamra is 100% alcohol-free. We serve no alcoholic drinks, and no wine, spirits or liqueur is used in any dish or dessert. Our French classics are cooked with stocks, juices and herbs instead.

        Halal

        All our beef and chicken are halal, from certified suppliers. There is no pork anywhere on our menu or in our kitchen: where a French classic calls for ham or bacon, we use halal smoked beef. Seafood, duck and snails are not marked halal on the menu.

        Food safety certificate

        Our kitchen follows HACCP food-safety practices, and our team is trained in food hygiene. The certificate is displayed at the entrance.
