Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Maison Hamra ("we", "us") runs this website and app so you can browse our menu, reserve a table, pay deposits and share feedback. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who handles it for us, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with Lebanese Law No. 81 of 2018 on Electronic Transactions and Personal Data.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, phone number, email address, password (stored encrypted)When you create an accountTo identify you, keep your reservation codes in your profile, and contact you about your bookings
Reservation details: date, time, number of guests, set menu, notes (for example allergies or an occasion)When you reserveTo hold your table, prepare for your visit and call you on the day
Deposit details: amount, payment method, the reference or receipt number you enterWhen you report a deposit paymentTo check your payment and secure your table
Attendance and payment history (no-shows, unpaid deposits)After each reservationTo manage bookings fairly; it is visible to our team only
Reviews and after-visit feedbackWhen you post themReviews are public with your first name and last initial; feedback is read by our team only
Phone alert registration (a technical address from your browser)When you turn on alertsTo send updates to your phone; it contains no personal content

We never collect or store card numbers. Card payments happen on our payment provider's secure page, and bank or Whish transfers go directly to our account.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in.

Who handles your data for us

  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.
  • Resend: sends our emails (reservation confirmations, password resets), once email is turned on.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla): delivers phone alerts.
  • Our payment provider and bank: process deposit payments you choose to make.

Some of these providers are outside Lebanon. They process data only to provide their service to us. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

  • Your account stays until you ask us to delete it.
  • Reservation, deposit and attendance records are kept for up to 2 years after the reservation date, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Reviews stay public until you delete them (you can do this yourself) or ask us to remove them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and data. You can also withdraw consent to phone alerts at any time in your browser settings. To make a request, call or WhatsApp us on +961 00 000 000, or ask at the restaurant. We reply within 30 days.

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Each customer can access only their own reservations and notifications, and only staff accounts can open the admin app. Passwords are stored encrypted and are never visible to our team.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 18 and over. Younger guests are welcome at the restaurant with an adult who makes the reservation.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
