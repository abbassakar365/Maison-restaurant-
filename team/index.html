                  Phone calls

                  The phone Auto-pilot answers the restaurant's number, books tables after the guest confirms the details, and puts the call through to the team phone when the guest asks for a person. Every call is listed below with what was said.

                  Use the international format, for example +961 3 123 456. The Auto-pilot switches to French or Arabic by itself when the guest speaks it.
