Terms of Use & Reservations

Last updated: 13 October 2026

These terms apply when you use the Maison Hamra website and app and when you reserve a table. By creating an account or making a reservation, you agree to them.

Your account

  • Give your real name and a phone number we can reach you on.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for reservations made from your account.
  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.

Reservations

  • We accept reservations every day from 12:00 to 23:00, from one hour to 90 days ahead.
  • A reservation is a request until our team confirms it. You get a notification when it is confirmed.
  • We hold your table for 15 minutes after the reserved time. If you are running late, please call us.
  • When we are fully booked at a time, that time can't be selected. For groups larger than the app allows, please call us.
  • You can hold up to 5 upcoming reservations at a time.
  • On the day, we may call you to give final approval.

Set menus and deposits

  • When a set menu order is over $300, a 25% deposit is required to secure the table.
  • The deposit must be paid before the deadline shown on your reservation. If it is not paid in time, the reservation is cancelled automatically.
  • The deposit is deducted from your final bill.
  • If you cancel at least 24 hours before your reservation, we refund the deposit in full. If you cancel later or do not come, the deposit may be kept.
  • If we cancel your reservation, we refund the deposit in full.
  • Card payments are processed by our payment provider. We never see or store your card number.

Cancellations and no-shows

  • You can cancel any upcoming reservation from your profile. Our team is notified straight away.
  • If you do not come and do not cancel, the reservation is recorded as a no-show. Repeated no-shows or unpaid deposits may limit future online bookings.
  • We may cancel a reservation in exceptional situations. We will notify you and tell you why.

Menu, allergies and dietary information

  • Our menu is pork-free and alcohol-free. Our beef and chicken are halal.
  • Ingredients are listed for each dish, but recipes can change and our kitchen handles common allergens (nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs, fish, shellfish, sesame). Tell us about allergies in your reservation notes and to your server.
  • Prices are in US dollars and may change. The price on the day of your visit applies.

Reviews and feedback

  • Reviews must be honest and about your own experience. No insults, hate, personal data of others or advertising.
  • We may remove reviews that break these rules.

Liability

We work to keep the app available and accurate, but it may sometimes be unavailable or contain errors. If something goes wrong with an online reservation, call us and we will do our best to help. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under Lebanese consumer protection law.

Governing law
